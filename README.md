## About the Project

This project explores Digital Lending Apps (DLAs) in India, explaining how digital lending works, its advantages and risks, and how the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulates digital lending activities.

The research focuses on the RBI (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025, borrower protection, financial transparency, data privacy, and responsible digital lending.

## Research Question

How do digital lending apps work in India, what benefits and risks do borrowers face, and how do RBI rules help students and other borrowers identify safe, regulated lending apps?

## Key Research Areas

1. Introduction to Digital Lending Apps.
2. Role of Banks, NBFCs and Lending Service Providers (LSPs).
3. RBI Digital Lending Directions, 2025.
4. Key Fact Statement (KFS) and Annual Percentage Rate (APR).
5. Benefits and risks of digital lending.
6. Borrower protection and data privacy.
7. AI research tools and verification of AI-generated information.
8. Recommendations for students and borrowers.

## Research Tools

- **NotebookLM:** Research and analysis of RBI and legal documents.
- **Perplexity AI:** Finding recent information, examples and references.
- **Claude AI:** Cross-checking research and improving report structure.
- **GitHub:** Project documentation, collaboration and version control.

## Key Findings

- Banks and NBFCs are the actual regulated lenders.
- Lending Service Providers (LSPs) act on behalf of regulated lenders.
- Borrowers should receive a Key Fact Statement before accepting a loan.
- APR helps borrowers understand the actual cost of borrowing.
- RBI's public digital lending app directory is not an RBI approval or guarantee.
- Borrowers have protections relating to data privacy and complaints.
